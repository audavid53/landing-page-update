Files are named by the **role they play in iPlace**, not by their emoji name, so a
commissioned or licensed 3D pack can replace them one file at a time without
touching any component. The slug -> upstream name mapping lives in
`scripts/emoji-manifest.mjs`; re-fetch with `pnpm assets:3d`.
